06.06.08 personal project

KICHKA

Collection

Kichka — the traditional headdress worn by married women across regions of Imperial Russia — is one of the most architecturally complex articles of folk dress ever constructed. Built from wire, fabric, and elaborate embroidered panels, each piece was made to be seen from a distance and understood up close.

This collection reinterprets its structural logic: the hidden armature, the layered textile surface, the way ornament and engineering are inseparable in the original form.
